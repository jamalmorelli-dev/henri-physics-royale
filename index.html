⚡ NEWTON'S 1ST LAW (INERTIA)

Objects stay moving unless an unbalanced force pushes back. On grass, soccer balls stop because of friction and drag. Cyclists shave body hair and wear aerodynamic spandex to eliminate air friction.

🚀 NEWTON'S 2ND LAW (F = ma)

Force = Mass × Acceleration. If mass doubles, you need 2× the force. LeBron weighs 245 lbs, so his legs must push with vastly more force into the court than a 170-lb player.

💥 NEWTON'S 3RD LAW (ACTION / REACTION)

Every action has an equal and opposite reaction. LeBron pushes DOWN on the floor (action); the floor pushes back UP against his feet (reaction). Muscle produces contractile force; fat is dead weight.

👑 THEORY VS. LAW (MR. CAROSAARI'S TRAP)

Laws describe WHAT happens (often math). Theories explain WHY it happens (predictive, repeatable). A Theory NEVER turns into a Law!
